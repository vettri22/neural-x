Databases Checked
  • DOAJ
    Directory of Open Access Journals — the gold standard for OA journal verification.
  • Crossref
    Publisher and DOI registry. Legitimate publishers register here for DOI assignment.
  • OpenAlex
    Open scholarly graph — indexes works from legitimate academic sources.
  • NOT VERIFIED ≠ FAKE
    If an external database is unreachable, we show NOT VERIFIED — never FAKE. Verify directly if needed.
  • Disclaimer
    All scores are heuristic estimates. Always verify independently at Think.Check.Submit.
Warning Signs
  • Rapid / guaranteed publication
  • Pay after acceptance
  • Unverifiable Scopus/WoS claims
  • No real editorial board
  • Missing ethics / retraction policy
  • Invalid or no ISSN
  • Very new domain (< 6 months)
  • No publisher in Crossref
